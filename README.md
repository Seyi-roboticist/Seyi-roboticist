## What I Work On

**Manipulation & Motion Control** — Real-time Cartesian control, singularity-robust inverse kinematics, ros2_control hardware interfaces. Sub-millimeter accuracy at 500Hz on UR5/UR5e.

**Flight Controls & GNC** — 6DOF modeling, successive loop closure autopilot design, dual EKF state estimation. Fixed-wing and multirotor platforms.

**Perception & Deep Learning** — NeRF for 3D reconstruction, computer vision pipelines, sim-to-real transfer with PyTorch.

**Autonomy & Navigation** — Full-stack ROS 2 autonomy from Gazebo simulation through real hardware deployment on aerial, ground, and underwater platforms.

**Industrial Automation** — Allen-Bradley PLC programming, electrical schematics, ladder logic for manufacturing lines. Bridging ROS 2 with legacy control systems.
